Trail Rations

Most races craft daily trail rations to be compact as possible and last as long as possible without molding or rotting. Any edibility is incidental. Trail rations generally include smoked jerky with the consistency of shoe leather, biscuits that can double as sling bullets, and bricks of fat mixed with berries and ground dried meat.

Elves, of course, forgo such foodstuffs and subsist off a mix of delectable dried fruits, cunningly preserved cheeses, and thin-baked bread that lasts for months without losing its light and fluffy texture. Without exception, travelers from other races resent them mightily for this.

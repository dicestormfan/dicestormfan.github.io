Removing Dice

Just like some effects add Boost dice [B] or Setback dice [S] to a pool, some effects remove dice from the pool before you even roll them. Most often, this is a result of talents that allow the removal of Setback dice [S] (for instance, the Knack for It talent on page 73). However, some talents or other effects may remove [B] from your skill checks as well; these are just a bit less common.

The individual talent or effect describes under what circumstances you remove the dice, as well as the number and type of dice you remove. If an ability calls for the removal of more dice of a type than there are in the dice pool, all of the dice of that type in the pool are removed, and any additional removals are ignored.

Remove dice after all other dice have been added and all upgrades and downgrades have been applied.

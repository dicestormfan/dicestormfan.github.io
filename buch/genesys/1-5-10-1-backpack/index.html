Backpack

This is a profile for a backpack. Backpacks can take a lot of different forms, but the basics of how one works remains the same. Backpacks vary in price and rarity, and they have one unique rule that lets characters carry more.

While wearing a backpack, your character increases their encumbrance threshold by four.

Stats: Encumbrance +4, Price 50, Rarity 3

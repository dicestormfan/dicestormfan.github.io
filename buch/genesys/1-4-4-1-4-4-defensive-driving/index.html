Defensive Driving

Tier: 4

Activation: Passive

Ranked: Yes

Increase the defense of any vehicle your character pilots by one per rank of Defensive Driving.

The specifics of this talent require the optional vehicle rules, on page 220. If your game does not use these rules, this talent adds [S] per rank to combat checks targeting your character's vehicle or your character while piloting it.

You should use this talent in the steampunk, weird war, modern day, science fiction, and space opera settings.

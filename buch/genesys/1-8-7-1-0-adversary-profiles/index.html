Adversary Profiles

Think of an adversary profile as a stripped-down version of one of your player characters' profiles. The goal is the same—to let the adversary function within the framework of Genesys's rules—even if the adversary's rules are a lot simpler than the PC rules. In Genesys, there are three types of adversary profiles. We call those three types minions, rivals, and nemeses.

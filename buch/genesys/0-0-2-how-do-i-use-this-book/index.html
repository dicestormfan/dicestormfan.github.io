How Do I Use This Book?

We designed Genesys to work with a wide variety of settings, instead of just one. This can be your science fiction roleplaying game, your fantasy roleplaying game, or even your "fighting airship pirates in Victorian England" game. We organized this book to facilitate these many options.

In Part I of this book, we cover the core rules of the game, the Narrative Dice System. These are the rules you'll need to know to play Genesys in any setting, from space opera to steampunk. With the exception of our chapter on how to be a Game Master (Chapter 8), Part 1 is the section that everyone should read.

In Part II of the book, we present five different example settings in which you could run a game of Genesys. This isn't an exhaustive list; that would require a much larger book! However, we wanted these example settings to show aspiring GMs what would go into creating their own setting, and give them some ideas to get started. This section is mostly for Game Masters.

In Part III, we present a whole lot of advice for GMs who want to build their own rules and flesh out their own settings. We also have some alternate rules that you don't need for all games of Genesys, but should be useful in some settings. This is our toolbox, and while this section is mostly for GMs, we expect players will want to read and learn some of these alternate rules.

You can create your own setting and run your own game just using the material in this book. However, we also designed it to be paired with setting supplements. A setting supplement presents a single setting (with new character options, items, adversaries, and environments) in more exhaustive detail, while relying on this book to provide the core rules for playing the game.

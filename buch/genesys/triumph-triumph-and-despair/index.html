Triumph

A Triumph symbol {Tr} has two effects. First, each Triumph symbol {Tr} counts as one Success symbol {S} . Second, a Triumph result indicates an unexpected boon or significantly beneficial effect related to the check your character just made. Many weapons and talents have side effects that can be triggered using a Triumph result. Otherwise, the scenario or your GM may present further options for using Triumph.

It's important to keep in mind that while a Triumph symbol {Tr} does add a Success symbol {S} to the pool, the presence of one or more Triumph symbols {Tr} does not automatically mean that the check succeeded.

You can think of Triumph as an enhanced, more powerful version of Advantage. For example, a single Triumph symbol {Tr} can trigger any weapon's special quality or inflict a Critical Injury, while without a Triumph, these effects require a certain number of Advantage symbols {A} . Also, while Advantage may allow your character to heal strain, in the right situation, a Triumph {Tr} may allow them to heal a wound.

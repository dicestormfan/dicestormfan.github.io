Characteristics in Play

Generally, most characters focus on some characteristics over others, which helps define who they are. Characteristics also influence skills, a wide variety of character abilities, and some derived statistics. For example, in addition to being used for Athletics and Melee skill checks, Brawn determines a character's starting wound threshold and forms the basis of a character's soak value.

As we mentioned before, characteristics are one of the four factors that affect the composition of a dice pool for a check (the other factors being skill training, task difficulty, and equipment and other incidental circumstances).

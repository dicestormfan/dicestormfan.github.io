Guided (Active)

A weapon with the Guided quality can track opponents. The Guided quality can only be triggered if an attack misses. If Guided is triggered, the controlling character may make a combat check at the end of the round as an out-of-turn incidental. The difficulty of this combat check is Average ([D][D]); instead of building the ability of the pool normally, add [A] equal to the weapon's Guided rating. If the check is successful, the weapon strikes the target, and the attack is resolved normally.

Guided requires {A}{A}{A} to activate, unless otherwise specified in the weapon's description. The Guided quality can activate on any subsequent combat check it makes, representing the projectile continuing to track the target.

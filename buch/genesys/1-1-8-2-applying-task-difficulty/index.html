Applying Task Difficulty

After determining which skill and related characteristic your character uses to attempt a task, your GM then chooses the level of difficulty for the task by consulting Table I.1–3: Difficulty Levels, on page 18. The difficulty level of the task determines the number of Difficulty dice you must add to the pool. For example, an Average ([D][D]) skill check means you add two Difficulty dice to the dice pool.

In some cases, your GM may upgrade one or more of these Difficulty dice by removing them from the dice pool and replacing them with an equal number of Challenge dice [C]. Difficulty dice are usually upgraded into Challenge dice when your character faces skilled opposition or particularly challenging circumstances, or when your GM invests a Story Point to make a check more challenging.

After your GM sets the difficulty level for the task, you add the corresponding number of Difficulty dice to the task's dice pool. If no other factors influence the outcome of the attempt, the basic dice pool is now complete, and you can roll it to determine success or failure, as well as any potential side effects.

Bought Info

Tier: 1

Activation: Active (Action)

Ranked: No

When making any knowledge skill check, your character can instead use this talent to spend an amount of currency equal to fifty times the difficulty of the check and automatically succeed on the knowledge check with one uncanceled {S} (instead of rolling). At your GM's

discretion, your character may not be able to use Bought Info if the information is particularly sensitive or difficult to find, or buying it doesn't make narrative sense.

You should use this talent in all settings.

Sources

Experience Points

Awarding experience points is covered on page 125, and it is another powerful resource. Receiving experience points during pivotal moments, upon unraveling a mystery, or after defeating a powerful enemy gives players a tangible sense of accomplishment. Providing small bonuses here and there can also be used to reward clever play, neat ideas, or excellent roleplaying.

Forgot to Count?

Tier: 3

Activation: Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)

Ranked: No

When an opponent makes a ranged combat check, you can spend {Th}{Th} from that check to use this talent to cause their weapon to run out of ammo (see page 104), as long as the weapon can normally run out of ammunition.

You should use this talent in the steampunk, weird war, modern day, science fiction, and space opera settings.

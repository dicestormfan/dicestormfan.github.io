Boost and Setback Dice

Two of the most useful tools available are the Boost [B] and Setback [S] dice. These dice replace countless charts and modifier tables with a simple way to reflect the context of a situation. Adding [B] or [S] dice to skill checks provides a tangible way to establish details of the story.

If it's cold outside, just how cold is it? Chilly enough to warrant mention? Cold enough that it impairs the characters by providing a [S]? Or so dangerously cold that [S][S] or more are added? [B] and [S] help establish the magnitude of an effect.

When players describe their PCs' actions or make interesting plans, you can reward this by providing [B] to represent the impact and importance of different parts of their descriptions. Similarly, [S] reinforce the severity of disruptive or distracting conditions.

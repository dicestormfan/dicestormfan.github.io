Successes and Failures

When you roll a skill check, if the results include both Success symbols {S} and Failure symbols {F}, count up the total number of each type of symbol and then compare those two numbers. Each Failure symbol {F} cancels one Success symbol {S}. After you make all the needed cancellations due to Failure symbols {F}, a pool of one or more Success symbols {S} or one or more Failure symbols {F} remains, or those symbols evenly cancel out.

During this step, it's important to note that a Triumph symbol {Tr} contributes one Success {S} to the pool, in addition to any special effects it may trigger. Likewise, a Despair symbol {D} contributes one Failure {F} to the pool, in addition to its special effects.

If all Successes {S} and Failures {F} in the pool are canceled out, or if any net Failures {F} remain, the skill check fails. If at least one Success {S} remains, the skill check succeeds. Remember, a dice pool must have at least one Success symbol {S} remaining for a skill check to succeed.

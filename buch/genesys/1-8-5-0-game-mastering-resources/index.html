Game Mastering Resources

Genesys puts a lot of resources at your fingertips. You can leverage these resources to reward players, reinforce important plot points, or help establish how the players' choices have in-game consequences.

  • Boost and Setback Dice
  • Story Points
  • Motivation
  • Strain
  • Initiative
  • Experience Points
  • Time and Timing

Step 5: Determine Derived Attributes

Once you've spent your character's starting XP, the next step is to determine your character's derived attributes. We call them derived attributes because they are determined by your character's other attributes and your choice of archetype or species.

  • Wound Threshold
  • Strain Threshold
  • Defense
  • Soak Value

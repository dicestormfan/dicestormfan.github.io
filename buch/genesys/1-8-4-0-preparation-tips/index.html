Preparation Tips

Genesys makes it easy for you to prepare for a game session, offering a number of quick tips and tricks to help you get ready. Game prep does not need to be a long, complex task. It can often be done with just a bit of time and something for taking notes, such as a pad of paper, some index cards, or a text program on a tablet.

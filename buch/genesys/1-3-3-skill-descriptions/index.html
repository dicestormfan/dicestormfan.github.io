Skill Descriptions

This chapter details all of the skills used throughout the game. Each skill comes with a basic description of what it is and some brief suggestions for times your character might use the skill and for some times when your character wouldn't use the skill.

When reading the description, remember that the Your character should (and shouldn't) use this skill if… sections are meant to be suggestions and guidelines, not exhaustive lists. They should spur your imagination and give you a frame of reference, not limit you.

Finally, each skill entry describes what settings that skill is most appropriate for. Your GM can always decide to use any skill in a setting, of course, but you should check with your GM before you invest in the Computers skill for a campaign in a steampunk setting, for example.

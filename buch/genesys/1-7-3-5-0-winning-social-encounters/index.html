Winning Social Encounters

While "winning" in a combat encounter is pretty straightforward (just defeat the opponents), how to "win" a social encounter can seem less clear. Obviously, characters want to accomplish their goal, but how you, the GM, can do that without making it seem like an arbitrary decision can be a bit trickier.

Given that, we have three suggestions as to how you can determine if a character accomplishes their goal successfully.

  • Proposing a Mutually Agreeable Solution
  • Succeeding on an Opposed Social Skill Check
  • Targeting the Opponent's Strain Threshold

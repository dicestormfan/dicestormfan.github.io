Overcharge (Improved)

Tier: 5

Activation: Passive

Ranked: No

Your character must have purchased the Overcharge talent to benefit from this talent. When using the Overcharge talent, your character may spend {A}{A} or {Tr} from the Mechanics check to immediately take one additional action. This talent can only be used once per check.

You should use this talent in the science fiction and space opera settings.

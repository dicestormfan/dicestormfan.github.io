Motivation

Few things motivate a character better than their own self-interest. Having a Motivation can create an immediate relationship, often with some sort of cause-andeffect impact. If you owe money to a loan shark, they're going to want it back. If you're motivated to protect your family, they reach out to you in their time of need. If you want to get rich, you're going to want to call in any favors owed, preferably in the form of money.

Bringing up a Motivation can help your players feel that their characters have a connection to the game world and that things happen offscreen. More goes on in the story than just what's described during a session.

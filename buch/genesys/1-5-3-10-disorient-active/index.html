Disorient (Active)

A weapon with Disorient can daze an opponent. When Disorient is triggered, one target hit by the attack is disoriented (see page 114) for a number of rounds equal to the weapon's Disorient rating. A disoriented target adds [S] to all skill checks they perform. If multiple targets suffer hits from a weapon with Disorient, the quality may be triggered multiple times, affecting a different target each time.

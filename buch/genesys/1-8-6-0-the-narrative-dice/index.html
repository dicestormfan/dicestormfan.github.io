The Narrative Dice

These special dice are a key feature of the game. They provide a wide range of possible outcomes, making each roll of the dice unique. The Narrative dice offer a lot of opportunity for enhancing the story with a deeper narrative, but they won't get in the way when you want to focus on fast-paced action.

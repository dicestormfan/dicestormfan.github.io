Acquiring Talents

Talents can be acquired by spending experience to purchase them. The cost of the talent depends on what tier that talent occupies. A talent costs five times the value of the talent's tier (so a Tier 1 talent costs 5 XP, and a Tier 2 talent costs 10 XP). We have some other perquisites and rules that govern purchasing talents, so we suggest checking out page 71 for more information.

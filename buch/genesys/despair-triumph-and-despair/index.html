Despair

A Despair symbol {D} also has two effects. First, each Despair symbol {D} counts as one Failure symbol {F} . Second, a Despair result indicates an unfortunate consequence, significant complication, or dire effect related to the check your character just made. The opponent's abilities, the environment, or the encounter description may offer different options for using Despair. Otherwise, your GM adjudicates the results of Despair symbols {D} based on the situation.

It's important to keep in mind that while a Despair symbol {D} does add a Failure symbol {F} to the pool, the presence of one or more Despair symbols {D} does not automatically mean the skill check has failed.

<Page 25>

You can think of Despair as an upgraded, more potent form of Threat. For example, a single Despair symbol {D} may trigger effects that would normally require several Threat symbols {Th} . Despair may be significant enough to inflict wounds instead of strain, cause an important piece of equipment to break, make a weapon jam or run out of ammo, or, when coupled with failure, make a grenade explode in the user's hand.

Runeforge

The Runeforge is the seat of the Runescribes' Guild in Thelgrim, a massive cruciform hall built partly into the cavern walls at the city's edge, housing the Dwarves' collection of known dragon runes.

Its four immense wings each serve a separate purpose: the south wing, where the entrances are located, holds offices and meeting chambers; the west wing contains the forges, casting molds, and foundries where new runic items are cast and created; the north wing houses two copies of each known dragon rune, cast from an appropriate metal and hammered into rock slabs; and a further wing, buried in the stone and pointing toward the Molten Heath, protects the complete records of Dwarven history etched onto beaten copper sheets. From the Runeforge, the runescribes can gaze out over the entire city — a reminder that they, more than anyone, are responsible for preserving the soul of Dunwarr's culture. Beyond maintaining that history and etching dragon runes into arms and armor, the runescribes are also charged with discovering new runes or rediscovering those lost with the destruction of the Karok Doum; the current Master Scribe, Dorgan Hammerfist, is said to be considering hiring adventurers to explore the ruins of the Dwarves' ancestral cities on the Molten Heath in search of ancient runecraft.

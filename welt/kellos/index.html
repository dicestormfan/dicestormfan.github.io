Kellos

Kellos, the Blazing Light of Life and Justice, also called the Red-Handed God, is the god of Life, Flame, and Justice, a fiery deity whose presence is anathema to anything tainted by the foul magic of undeath or other dark sorceries.

Origins

Two differing accounts of Kellos's origin survive. The Grand Hierophants of Kellos hold that, soon after the Second Darkness, a vile necromancer near Vynelvale sought to create an undead empire for himself; a band of heroes assembled to defeat him but were struck down, and the last survivor, moments from a sorcerous death, called out for aid and swore eternal service to any who would come. His eyes clouded, his body wreathed in flame, and he smote the necromancer and banished his undead army forever — this survivor became the first Hierophant of Kellos and, upon his death, the church's first saint.

Historians and religious legend alike instead place his rise during the height of Waiqar's Scourge, when the Deathborn Legion laid waste to Terrinoth and the Death Cults spread fear and disorder through the nation's cities like an evil plague. By this account, a stranger arrived by boat at the refugee camp of Dawnsmoor, introducing himself as Kellos and working miracles of healing among the settlers there. He took up his mace and swore to see an end to the vile undead, and with his holy fire burned the flesh and bone of Waiqar's foul creations; he called many brave adventurers to his side, and when they fell in defense of the realm, he saved them from death with his powers of healing, bidding them rise again from the flame. Kellos the Hero was soon lauded as Kellos the Bright and Kellos the Savior, one of the brightest lights of humankind amid the darkness that had washed over the land — within weeks he was the central figure of a burgeoning cult, surrounded by priests who could perform minor versions of his own miracles, and many claimed he had himself recovered from death after being struck down by an assassin's blade. Kellos freed the baronial forces from the need to guard Terrinoth's cities by concentrating on locating and executing the ringleaders of the death cults, and sent envoys to beseech the Latari Elves and Dunwarr Dwarves for aid against Waiqar; with their combined might, the Deathborn Legion was finally forced to retreat to the Cursed Barony in 521.

At the final battle, when Waiqar's armies were driven back to the Mistlands, Kellos disappeared — perhaps slain in combat, making a final sacrifice for the people and lands he loved, or perhaps emerging unscathed to settle in some far-off hamlet and enjoy peace long denied him. His followers believe he ascended to godhood that day, so that he might forever empower those who would fight death with the light and life of fire; his disciples returned to Vynelvale, now a Free City, and lit the Eternal Fire in his name. As they built a cathedral to their founder, Kellos's followers taught that heat and life were inextricably intertwined, and that magic which utilized heat and fire could also be used to heal — soon many citizens of Vynelvale and the surrounding lands had been blessed with Kellos's healing gifts, and tales of these wondrous miracles spread across Terrinoth, with the most pious and powerful priests even said to bring the dead back to life through resurrection. It is said, too, that he still travels the world to this day, seeking new foes to defeat, and those in Vynelvale patiently await his eventual return to Terrinoth.

Though there is much debate whether Kellos was merely a hero or truly a god — some Greyhaven scholars hold that the magics of his priests are not divinely sourced at all, but mislabeled applications of the Turning or accidental invocations of the Empyrean — none deny that his priests are worthy allies in the fight to keep Terrinoth safe from the terrors that lurk in the dark.

The Church of Kellos

Those who join the Church of Kellos are called Acolytes, low-ranking clergy who tend his flames and flocks in shrines across Terrinoth. Those Acolytes who have the ability to perform divine feats in Kellos's name are initiated through the rite of the Fire's Embrace and become Priests or Knights of Kellos, bringing healing to towns and villages or traveling the roads to smite evil wherever it arises. The most powerful of Kellos's followers are his High Priests, who preside over the cathedral at Vynelvale; High Priests called Justicars lead contingents of the priesthood into battle in the most dire times, among them Lady Anastel during the War of Ten Brothers. In battle, Kellos's priests most often use the power of flame, the symbol of their deity, to smite evil — conjuring mystical flame around hand or weapon, or casting gouts of living flame to consume their foes; their brilliant red robes are a welcome sight across Terrinoth, promising wrath for the wicked and mercy for the meek. Kellos's faith is centered on Vynelvale, site of the Grand Cathedral of Kellos and the Eternal Fire.

Some temples of Kellos teach that the undead are the agents of his twin brother Nordros, and that Nordros's power will only be broken, and winter forever banished, once Kellos returns.

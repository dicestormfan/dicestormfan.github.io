The First

The First are the entities from whom even mighty gods owe their own existence, greater than gods in potential yet lesser for lacking personality and form — neither singular nor plural, neither male nor female, existing before there was barons, wars, earth, stars, or time.

Description

The legends are not clear as to whether the Void came into being with the First or the First created the Void; some interpretations suggest they are one and the same, and whatever the truth, the Void is the space within which all later creation took place. Without breath, tongue, or lips, the First are said to have uttered songs or poems — the Lays of the First — that shaped the Void, defining its potential and giving birth to all things; these verses described ideal forms and natures, and Elves insist that echoes of the Lays can still be heard in the rhythms of nature, nourishing spirits like food, in the tradition that the Wealdweavers work their craft.

The First conjured three distinct existences from the Void, called the Planes of Power: the highest, the Empyrean, a plane of perfection; the lowest, the Ynfernael, populated by monstrous entities that crave to visit torture and degradation upon mortals; and between them, the Aenlong, to which the plane of physical existence is connected. The First then set into motion the Verto Magica, without which everything would be fixed in place and time would stand still.

According to Emorial, in his final rebellion against the First, they were tyrants, afraid of being usurped by their own children — the false hope offered to Latariana was merely a means to keep the Elves subservient and obsequious for eternity. The Latari Elves who followed Malcorne came to share this view, styling their rebellion as a war against the First's tyranny.

Daewyl

The Daewyl, or "Twice Fallen" Elves, are the followers of Malcorne of the Malcari who, believing the lie of a demon wearing Emorial's face, corrupted their Tears of Latariana and allied with demons of the Ynfernael in The War of the Shadow Tear.

History

Malcorne's followers ravaged and routed the first Elven armies sent against them, scattering the other tribes before what they called the Daewyl, and the surviving Elves fled to remote regions, distant islands, and lonely mountaintops to protect their precious Tears from capture. Daewyl sorcerers opened up many rifts between the mortal realm and the Ynfernael, and any demon wishing to enter Mennara could use them to cross over into the vulnerable world; the Elves strove to locate the rifts and close them, sealing them with powerful wards of Empyrean magic, yet they never found them all. It was during this time that other races, driven into being through the mutating forces of Ynfernael power, came to make their mark on the world — the Dwarves, Orcs, and Humans among them.

Malcorne was ultimately struck down at The Battle of Enfreil, and the victors hunted down the remaining Daewyl to burn them from the face of the world, nearly extinguishing the Malcari in the slaughter. Many worry that small enclaves of the Daewyl still lurk in the dark nooks of the world, but at least one group, now calling themselves the Deep Elves, claims to have come back to the light, seeking to atone for their sins by destroying demons in this realm and others beyond.

Legacy

Maegan Cyndewin's father was a member of the Daewyl, a legacy that fuels much of the High Court's distrust of her, in her covetous pursuit of Latariana's Tears. The Daewyl also appear to draw on Ynfernael magic in more recent times: these Elves had dared to travel to blighted planes of the Aenlong to slay the many corrupted creatures there, but were themselves corrupted by the Ynfernael taint they found; many of the Daewyl now call upon that dark source of magic as they single-mindedly fight against evil, no matter the consequences to themselves or those around them.

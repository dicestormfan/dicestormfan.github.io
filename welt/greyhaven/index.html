Greyhaven

Greyhaven is one of the eight Free Cities of Terrinoth, home to its hallowed university — though few realize it was the town that came first and not the other way around.

History

Though records of its founding are sketchy, most hold that it was Timmorran himself who founded the northern city, perhaps for refugees lost in the wilderness and those seeking to forget the horrors of the First Darkness, though he may have had other reasons for the location. The nearby confluence of two rivers and the town's accessibility made it a natural trading nexus, and it grew over the years into a cosmopolitan center of learning; legends grew alongside it, such as that the Fae were repelled by the nearby waters, or that the undead could not be raised within its gates. Though never proven, these tales drew those of an arcane bent, and the greatest university in Terrinoth was built here solely to study magic, with deep dungeons excavated for dangerous experiments and the wisest wizards from every court and city recruited as instructors and masters.

Description

Greyhaven now boasts the foremost magical colleges in all of Terrinoth, perhaps in all the world, its wizards famed for their wisdom and dedication to the magical arts. The Daqan Lords draw their battlemages from Greyhaven, and most current knowledge of rune magic originates from it; the university possesses the largest number of known runebound shards of any city in Terrinoth, frequently used in its mages' more exotic and powerful experiments. Its colleges cover all the arts, from history to farming to rhetoric, though most specialize in aspects of magical lore and practice — attending means long days of study, long nights of reading, and frequent practice sessions that only rarely lead to catastrophic explosions, while graduation ensures a long, if dangerous, career. Even those not attending often travel here just to observe lectures and practice with adepts.

The Pilgrim's Trail through the Shadow Peaks lies not far from Greyhaven.

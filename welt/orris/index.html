Orris

Orris lies on the northern coast of Lorimor, where the turbulent River of Sighs empties into the Cerridor Sea — a thriving destination point, its docks busy day and night as heavy ships fight for space to offload their treasures from distant lands.

Its success has made it feel detached from the rest of Lorimor, though, and the city has an unseemly reputation: its bars and taverns became havens for captains with a piratical bent, and many an eye is averted so long as gold and other riches continue to pour into Lorimor. The colonization of The Torue Albes was launched from Orris, and the city controlled much of the trade with the islands as the settlements prospered and grew. Many call it a city of pirates — not an entirely untrue statement, but Orris is also a city of opportunity, its ships ready for any action as long as the promise of reward is high.

Aris

Aris, Patron of Peace and Prosperity, appeared as a traveler in the cold grey years following the Third Darkness, when Terrinoth lay in ruins and hope was in short supply.

History

Arriving at a small, half-burnt hamlet one midwinter's eve, Aris was welcomed by villagers who warned her they had little food left even for themselves. She told them not to worry, and from her pack pulled grain, meats, and hearty roots that somehow fed the whole village with enough left for the next morning as well. In the weeks that followed, she showed them where to find stores of nuts and berries in the woods, helped rebuild their homes against the cold, and even convinced a band of attacking bandits to settle down and give up their marauding ways. Then she left, stomping off into the snow with her pack on her back.

Many areas in Terrinoth tell similar tales, though if even a fraction are true she must have wandered for a hundred years. Those she aided built humble shrines to her and began leaving gifts in hopes of her blessing and protection; even rogues learned to leave those offerings alone, for any who stole them suffered a multitude of misfortunes for the rest of their lives. Only those with generous hearts could take and redistribute the gifts to those in greatest need, and in time these few began to call themselves her priests.

Worship

Aris's priests work tirelessly to help the poor and continue her works and convictions, mediating disputes from arguments between neighbors to wars between barons; while they may take up arms to defend the helpless, they always see violence as a last resort, and a personal failing on their part. Aris's largest shrine can be found near the Weeping Basin, where the body of the Dragonlord Margath is said to have fallen to earth after being slain — a special order within her priests, the Watchers, guard the site to ensure Margath never rises again.

Orb of the Sky

The Orb of the Sky was Timmorran's final and most ambitious creation, a huge crystalline sphere of pure magic meant to contain a portion of the Verto Magica's own energy, designed as a reservoir of magical power so potent that wizards who came after him would be able to use it to power countless spells with relative ease and little risk of Ynfernael corruption.

History

At the Tower of Meringyr, Timmorran and his acolytes strove to create the Orb, and he invested so much of his own energy into the artifact that he grew visibly infirm — his emaciation shocked those present at a memorial service for King Daqan in Archaut in 513. When Waiqar launched his assault on the Wizard's Vale during the Night of Betrayal in 515, Timmorran realized his folly in making the Orb and cast spells of unbinding, hurling the crystal sphere to the forge floor so powerfully that it shattered. Using magic, he channeled the myriad pieces into an enchanted velvet pouch and entrusted it to Lumii Tamar, having him swear to distribute the fragments throughout the world to those he deemed wise enough to use them — through a feat of high magic, Lumii slipped past Waiqar's forces with all but a single fragment. That last piece Waiqar tore from the dead hands of the wizard he had just slain, vowing to hunt down the rest.

Legacy

The fragments of the Orb, kept secret and safe by Lumii Tamar and the order he was granted authority to found, came to be known as the Stars of Timmorran — unstable magical artifacts capable of powering great magic in a trained caster's hands. When the dragonlords of the Third Darkness broke these Stars down into smaller, stable runebound shards, they transformed the nature of magic across Mennara.

Loth K'har

The Loth K'har were the peaceful, nomadic plains-dwellers of The Ru before Llovar fell prey to the demons and transformed them into the Uthuk Y'llan, the hungry horde of locusts, on the Night of Summons.

History

During good times, the Loth K'har roved the plains, searching for fresh grazing grounds for their animals and trading with the many settlements that ringed the wastes; in leaner times, they would turn into vicious raiders, falling on those same settlements for loot. Their shamans learned of a realm known as the T'mara T'rusheen, and from it gleaned prophetic advice; the most skillful among these shamans formed a caste known as the nightseers, said to have been close to members of a distant Elven tribe so impressed by their talents that they called them the Loth Caara, Elven for "dream walkers" — from this term the Loth K'har derive their own name. Llovar himself, born in 412, was among the most gifted of these nightseers, earning the title by his eleventh birthday before his corruption in the T'mara T'rusheen set the tribe's transformation in motion.

As the Ynfernael's power corrupted and transformed their flesh, it twisted the magics of the Loth K'har as well: the nightseers of their ancient dreamwalking tradition, who once used their abilities to travel to and commune with benevolent spirits, now used them to travel to and commune with demons from beyond, and their ancient ability to link minds into harmonious discussion grew darker until it became their method to command and dominate the Locust armies. Those who spoke out against the corruption of their ancient ways were destroyed, leaving only rage and hate in their place.

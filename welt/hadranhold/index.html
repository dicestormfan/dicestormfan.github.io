Hadranhold

Hadranhold is a Dwarven fortress-city in the Dunwarr Mountains, built into Mount Valborne, that grew from a lonely watchpost into a fortress and then a city over the centuries.

From the surface, all that can be seen of Hadranhold is the original stone tower and battlements built into the mountain's peak; the Rangers' Guild maintains the tower as its unofficial headquarters. The rest of the city lies deep below the surface, reached from the tower by a single stone staircase of thousands of steps, and has been carved into the caverns of the Deeps much as Thelgrim was. Unlike Thelgrim, Hadranhold lies close to the molten heart of Mennara, and members of the Smiths' Guild and Artificers' Guild have moved there in numbers to take advantage of the abundant heat for smelting and forging; the lower city, unlike the cold and lonely upper fortress, has become a bustling city of skilled artisans.

Deep below Hadranhold sits the Firestone Furnace, an immense, ancient kiln forged of an unnamed alloy standing in the center of a lake of boiling magma. Every winter solstice, Dwarves of the Rangers' Guild from across the Northern Coast make the trek to Hadranhold for Wintersmeet, the Guild's traditional week of feasting and fellowship.

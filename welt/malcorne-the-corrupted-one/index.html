Malcorne, the Corrupted One

Malcorne was the proud bearer of a Tear of Latariana and chieftain of the Malcari, the eleventh Elven tribe, whose corruption plunged the Elves into The War of the Shadow Tear — a stain the Deep Elves have dedicated their endless crusade against the Ynfernael to redeeming.

History

The Malcari ventured forth from the boughs of The Aymhelin and roamed far from the other tribes, settling at last in the Jornall Mountains, where Emorial was believed to have pursued the Ynfernael's servants after abandoning the Elves. Among the other tribes, Malcorne's folk had a mixed reputation — hailed as heroes for carrying Emorial's legacy into the present, but also regarded as aloof and criticized for taking action without consulting the other tribes, having begun to study and use the demons' very power against them in their efforts to fight the Ynfernael.

During one patrol, Malcorne was leading a band of demon hunters deep into the caverns when their battles caused a cave-in that separated him from the others; he was thought to have perished alongside the treacherous demons, and his tribe wept for their lost leader. When he returned to the Malcari city, having somehow miraculously survived his encounter with a demon, Malcorne was a changed chieftain, given to brooding moods and regarding his fellows with suspicious glances. In time, he gathered a small coterie of his most trusted allies and explained that in his wanderings he had found Emorial himself, who revealed to him that the Tears of Latariana were a gift in disguise, powerful enough to open a door to the Aenlong and allow the Elves to return to their rightful home — and that the promise the Yrthwright Mennara had given Latariana was a lie, meant only to keep the Elves subservient and obsequious toward the First for the rest of eternity. In his dying breath, this Emorial charged Malcorne with revealing the truth to the other tribes and leading them in rebellion against their former masters. The Latari Elves maintain to this day that it was not Emorial whom Malcorne encountered in the cave, but a cunning demon wearing his face.

The Shadow Tear

Knowing the Elves would need strong allies to cast down the First, Malcorne used his Tear to pull down the shields between the Firma Dracem and the Aenlong, finding there a horde of demons who had crossed over from where the Ynfernael overlapped with the Aenlong. In that moment, the Tear of Latariana that Malcorne possessed grew dull and shadowy, losing the light it once held, becoming the Shadow Tear. Once he had corrupted his birthright, Malcorne used it to open up rents in the fabric of reality, though the process was arduous; he reached out to the other tribes, explaining Mennara's supposed lie and that their Tears were needed to break the shackles of reality and gather allies from the Aenlong. The other tribes saw Malcorne's plans for what they were — evil and corrupt, twisted by the whisperings of demons — and the two sides could not be reconciled. Malcorne launched an attack on the other Elven tribes, determined to capture their Tears by force, and backed by ranks of savage demons and packs of Ynfernael creatures, his followers ravaged and routed the first few Elven armies sent to stop them. Soon the other tribes scattered before Malcorne and what they called the Daewyl, or "Twice Fallen" Elves, and the surviving Elves fled to remote regions, distant islands, and lonely mountaintops to protect their precious Tears from capture.

Downfall

In the depths of darkness, Glaciel Snowstar, son of the chieftain of the tenth tribe, the Nivalis, refused to give in to despair after witnessing Malcorne's devastation firsthand. He rescued his father's Tear and traveled to the Yrthwrights' own home to ask the dragons for guidance, gathering companions along the way: High Priestess Celeneth of the Salish, the Feredel sorcerer Keldarim, Erenil the Swift of the Latari, and others. The dragons, tasked by the First with preserving the world, at first rebuffed Glaciel's appeal, believing the Elves would need to bear this burden of their own doing — interceding, they warned, would one day force the tribes to pay a terrible price. Glaciel would not give up, and accepted the dragons' terms; he and his companions returned to rally their tribes with a flight of dragons at their back, and on the Plains of Enfreil they fought a final battle against Malcorne, in the world's most desperate hour ultimately achieving victory and striking down the dark Elven lord.

The remaining Daewyl retreated from the combined Elven and draconic forces, hoping to hide themselves away, but the victors hunted down their foes to burn them from the face of the world, nearly extinguishing Malcorne's wretched tribe in the slaughter. Many worry that small enclaves of the Daewyl still lurk in the dark nooks of the world, but at least one group, now calling themselves the Deep Elves, claims to have come back to the light, seeking to atone for their sins by destroying demons in this realm and others beyond.

The Shadow Tear itself — also called the Shadow War, this conflict's alternate name — went missing during the fighting; if it survived unbroken, a powerful artifact combining both Empyrean and Ynfernael magic still waits somewhere for someone to find it, akin in power to the unbound Stars of Timmorran that the original Tears of Latariana are believed to have anticipated.

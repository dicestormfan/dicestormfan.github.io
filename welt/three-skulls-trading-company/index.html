Three Skulls Trading Company

Of all the merchant concerns that plunder the wealth of Zanaga, none are as large and powerful as the Three Skulls Trading Company — a veritable army unto itself, controlled by the pirate lords of Orris in the Torue Albes, peopled by every race imaginable.

In many ways, Three Skulls is all that passes for a non-native government in Zanaga; its distinctive triple-skull emblem can be seen on most exported goods as well as flying above settlements as far inland as Terr's Crossing. Motivated solely by a chance to make more coin, the local guild captains of Three Skulls are more than happy to help out travelers and adventurers, often funding their expeditions and even offering them guides, mercenaries, and discounts on goods — though such deals always come at a price, for canny guild captains will never strike a bargain if it is not in their best interests. Adventurers who are careless when it comes to reading the fine print may find themselves returning from an expedition owing the captain more treasure than they recovered, putting them in debt to Three Skulls, and once a person is in debt, a guild captain will do their best to keep them there. Many of the company's guild captains secretly serve the spider goddess Arachne.

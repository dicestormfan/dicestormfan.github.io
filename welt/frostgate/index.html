Frostgate

Frostgate is the northernmost of the Free Cities of Terrinoth, the entry point to the wild, untamed lands of eternal winter, and one of the few places in the region where raw materials can be procured and goods manufactured.

Barely a city for much of the year, Frostgate is more a seasonal community whose fortunes rise and fall with the coming of winter: summers see it almost emptied as its denizens leave to hunt and farm, but as the cold returns the city blooms, filling with hunters and trappers, herders and farmers, tradespeople and their retinues who return to meet and conduct business. The natural riches of the Blind Muir Forest, the shipping lane of the River of Tears, and the abundant minerals of the nearby Shadow Peaks feed its trade. During the crowded winter months, fields of yurts press against the city walls and industry pushes on into the night; in this season Frostgate can rival Tamalir in size, with temporary theaters, courts, temples, and shops erected within days, and the revelry can be heard long before the city is seen. The Northern Tribes perform rituals at this time to ensure good harvests and successful hunts, greatest among them the Trials of Frostgate — contests using sacred elixirs and herbs to access ancestral spirits, in which contestants enter spirit planes to face challenges of strength, cunning, and courage against aspects of mighty animals; those who succeed are marked with tattoos proclaiming them Champions of Frostgate. Before venturing into the North, bands of travelers tend to assemble in Frostgate, the sole community in the region outside a small colony of Latari among the evergreens, and the Champion's tattoo can be a sign to such groups that its bearer has adequate prowess for the dangers ahead.

Frostgate falls within the bounds of the Barony of Forthyn, which leads to some tension: the barony cannot officially command the Free City and its fiercely independent ruling council, but Frostgate relies on Forthyn's goodwill for its imports. The city also lies at the far end of the Pilgrim's Trail through the Shadow Peaks, where survivors of the trail's trials often travel onward to find the warmth of Frostgate's chimneys, roasting tundra boar, and Dwarven ale. Caravans travel between Frostgate and Thelgrim in the Dunwarr Mountains, inspected along the way by Bradha the Shield, commander of the garrison at the Gates of Thelgrim.

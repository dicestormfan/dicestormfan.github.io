Harriet the Willful

Harriet the Willful is the Baroness of Frest, a former carpenter named Harriet Laurel who rose from the peasantry to unseat the barony's squabbling nobility and take its throne.

Overview

Some decades before the present day, the baroness of Frest abruptly abdicated her position — Realms of Terrinoth records this earlier abdication as that of Baroness Georgiana Delacroux, who left to marry the orc chieftain Lazra the Huntress — leaving no named heir and throwing the barony into chaos that fell hardest on the peasantry, as various relatives vied for control. When the other barons treated the resulting succession conflict as sport, backing rival claimants, the carpenter Harriet Laurel grew tired of the fighting and led a rebellion of Frest's peasants and merchants.

History

The rebellion grew quickly in strength under Harriet's capable leadership; before the neighboring baronies could muster their levies, she had eliminated most of the legitimate claimants and taken control of the barony's major towns. She and her generals ruled under siege for fourteen years, repulsing every attempt by the other barons to put down the "peasant uprising." Eventually the barons relented and treated with her at Archaut, and under the neutral eyes of the Warden of the Citadel she was granted title and domain of Frest, becoming Baroness Harriet the Willful. The other barons then went to war with Frest outright, a conflict that raged for years until Harriet was finally accepted onto the Council of Thirteen, in no small part because the war was going badly for her opponents.

Since ruling from Aerendor Keep, Harriet has governed magnanimously, letting farmers keep what they like and giving Frest the lowest taxes in Terrinoth. Though other barons resent her common heritage, none can argue she has not ruled Frest justly. As she ages, the question of succession looms: should her daughter inherit and establish a hereditary line, some barons fear it would legitimize a precedent for other commoners to ennoble themselves or challenge baronial rule, and Baroness Magrit Kalrif of Dhernas and Baron Gilliam Xarles of Cailn have begun quietly seeking ways to turn Frest's people against her rather than risk open war or provoking revolt among their own peasants.

Geist

Geist, one of the ten sons of Baron Deiterhelm of Otrin, had long ago left the barony and renounced any claim of inheritance to study in Nerekhall — what he learned in that cursed city is unknown, but two years into the War of Ten Brothers he returned at the head of an army of vicious Ferrox raised from the wildlands of the Broken Crags.

History

Geist quickly crushed one of the three warring alliances of brothers, personally slaying two of his half-siblings; the others decried him as a "Usurper" and pled for aid against his obviously evil magics. When the barons refused to answer the call of the Warden of the Citadel, Lady Anastel of Kellos's Order was anointed Justicar to combat the threat, retrieving the Shield of Coals and marching alongside a force of zealous priests and knights — but Geist's magics warned him of her approach, and he ambushed her forces as soon as they crossed the border. Most who did not fall were corrupted and turned by the foul bite of the ferrox, barely one in four living to return to Vynelvale; Anastel was not among them, and Geist pried the Shield of Coals from her dead hands.

Geist seemed unstoppable, and in the winter of 1662 he laid siege to the fortress of Black Rock, a victory that would let him dominate the Morshan River all the way from Skydown to the Aymhelin forest. Bearing the Shield of Coals, he used its power to summon a hail of fire onto the defenders, and it fell to the free city of Riverwatch to stand alone against him — the entire company of the Riverwatch Riders mustered forth during the coldest days of Deepwinter and attacked his flanks before his horde, deployed to surround Black Rock, could turn to meet them. The ferrox faltered before the steel-eyed riders and fled with Geist to the frozen river, but as they crossed, the Shield in Geist's hands began to glow with searing heat; with a howl he dropped it on the ice, which melted away in the face of Kellos's wrath, and in moments Geist and his ferrox drowned in the freezing water.

Riverwatch

Riverwatch is one of the eight Free Cities of Terrinoth, a harbor city watched over by the tremendous statue known as the Old Man, standing on a high cliff with lantern in hand — a fitting symbol for the city's place in Terrinoth's history.

Initially founded as a military outpost of the Penacor Kings, Riverwatch has since risen to become one of the most self-sufficient of the Free Cities, dedicated to the defense of the southern continent. Its arguable seat of power is the Watch, which strategically overlooks Southern Terrinoth — more than a castle or garrison, a cultural institution and sign of the city's strength and martial history, the first building constructed on the site of what became Riverwatch, whose garrison took its name as their duty. It is here that riders are trained in horsemanship and swordfighting, and a special unit acts in a vital role even in peacetime, maintaining a messenger service reaching the far corners of Terrinoth: the Riverwatch Riders, who have proven the value of fast, reliable communication in ending many a threat before it becomes calamity. To the south lies the fortress of Sundergard, which often serves as their remote base of operations against dangers from the Mountains of Despair. Riverwatch sees healthy business thanks to the intricate chain of traders and merchants needed to supply its thousands of horses and riders, and a large contingent of Orcs, originally from the Broken Plains, calls the city home, their knowledge of taming wild beasts and animal husbandry proving invaluable.

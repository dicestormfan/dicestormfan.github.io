Falladir Penacor

King Falladir Penacor ruled Talindon during the First Darkness, and Terrinoth's capital city of Archaut later grew up around his burial site.

History

When Llovar's Uthuk Y'llan marched to despoil the southern lands in 478, Falladir mustered his soldiers to face them but was comprehensively bested in open battle; Nashaia struck him from his steed and was poised to kill him before Prince Parren's desperate counterattack rescued the king. While Falladir nursed his wounds, the tattered remnants of his army became little more than a fugitive band, and his efforts to restore law and order to his ravaged realm continued under the constant threat of Llovar's return. Aid came the following year when a force of Latari Elves under King Triamlavar marched from the Aymhelin to fight alongside the Penacors, beginning a great tradition of alliance between Humans and Elves against Ynfernael threats; together their combined forces relieved Thelgrim from siege, and Falladir and Triamlavar later joined Timmorran's campaign against Llovar. In 487, the Host of Thorns fell upon an army led by Falladir and his sons at The Ashen Field; though Falladir's sword nearly cleaved the head from Melinesh, Llovar's numbers, magic, and malice proved too great, and the three Penacors lay dead upon the field by the day's end, along with the soldiers who had followed their banner. Only a small group of survivors led by the young Baron Daqan escaped the slaughter.

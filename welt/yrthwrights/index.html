Yrthwrights

The Yrthwrights, also called the Foundrakes, are the first dragons and the creators of Mennara; dragons see themselves as the inheritors of the Yrthwrights' legacy, understanding their role as stewards of Mennara and guardians of its inhabitants — at least, most of them do.

History

The Dragonkin must have existed before The First created the mortal realm, since the Dragonkin played a part in its construction, molded from the debris of creation and granted the ability to speak matter into existence in a manner reminiscent of the Lays of the First — though Runemasters of Greyhaven disagree as to whether the runes of the dragons are vestiges of that same language. Through their incantations, the first dragons set about creating the Firma Dracem, the mortal realm, and adorned it with spinning bodies, planets, and suns; for this great work, nothing less than existence as mortals perceive it, they were termed the Foundrakes, also called the Yrthwrights. One of their number, the Yrthwright Mennara, architected the glade that became The Aymhelin and passed judgment upon the rebellious Elves who stumbled into it, exiling them to the very world that some dare name in Mennara's honor.

Description

A number of dragons throughout history have seen the mortals of Mennara as potentially useful tools in the battle to protect the Yrthwrights' creation from the depredations of the Ynfernael and the abyssal entities that dwell within.

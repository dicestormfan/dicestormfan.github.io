Labyrinth of Deurg

The Labyrinth of Deurg is a sealed and forbidden network of mines in the Dunwarr Mountains, named for Master Miner Deurg, whose team from the Miners' Guild discovered it many centuries ago while following an unusual seam of black, obsidian-like ore toward the mountain's surface.

Overview

The Smiths' Guild determined that the ore, once smelted, produced a shining metal of unparalleled strength and resilience, and Deurg redoubled his miners' efforts to excavate it. Before long the miners began to lose their bearings underground — a phenomenon entirely new to Dwarves — digging for days without seeming to move, or finding the richest deposits exactly where they expected the seam to end. Eventually they broke into unexpected caves and chambers lined with razor-sharp outcroppings of the black stone and threaded with strands of the strange metal.

History

Then the whispering started. The miners had been hearing it for weeks before realizing it was more than excitement in their own heads: a voice urging them to "dig," promising that the most valuable lodes lay just ahead. Reports of the whispers were the last word to leave the mines. When search parties from Thelgrim found the tunnel entrances caved in, the Dwarves dug through, but found no trace of the missing miners inside. The reigning Deeplord ruled that all ingots of the strange metal be returned to the mine, which was then sealed forever behind runes of warding and binding, and all exploration of what Dwarves now call the Labyrinth of Deurg was forbidden on pain of death.

The prohibition has held for hundreds of years, though occasionally a band of foolhardy adventurers is seduced by tales of the mythical ore; all who have found a new entrance into the Labyrinth have vanished within its depths forever. Persistent rumors also claim that a young blacksmith once spirited away a portion of the metal before it could be returned, forged it into a deadly blade, and went adventuring, only to be lost in the Woods of Woe.

The tale of the Labyrinth's discovery is also linked in Dwarven memory to the Pact of Stonesense, which holds that the Dwarves' singular affinity with the Dimora began when the first Dwarves, fleeing deeper into the mountains to escape the dragons, encountered the Dimora in the Dunwarr's very roots.

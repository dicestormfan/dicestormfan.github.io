Timmorran

Timmorran Lokander, son of Llander and Rala and nephew of Llovar, was one of the great heroes of the First Darkness, a wizard whose campaign against his uncle and the Uthuk Y'llan culminated in the Locust Lord's death, and who is credited with founding the Free City of Greyhaven.

Early Life

Born to fugitive parents fleeing Llovar's wrath on the Night of Summons, Timmorran was orphaned by the age of five and struggled as a friendless beggar on the streets of Lorimor, his untrained talent for magic already apparent in street sorcery performed for astonished crowds. A conclave of local wizards inducted him into their order, and he was later apprenticed to the celebrated wizard Sal Meringyr, who planted in him a dangerous ambition to crystallize the Verto Magica's own energy. When assassins of Q'aro Fenn sent by Llovar finally tracked Timmorran down, Sal Meringyr died defending him; Timmorran then fled across the seas to the far-off continent of Ghom, making a new life for himself in Al-Kalim, also known as the Sunderlands, where he offered his services to the tribe of Ashan the Elder and grew into an ever more potent magician and adventurer, as well as a friend to one Waiqar Sumarion.

The First Darkness

In the year 481, Timmorran persuaded Ashan the Elder to send an army of Sundermen to the defense of Talindon against Llovar's invasion, reasoning that if the Uthuk could not be stopped there, war would eventually come to his own homeland. The plan took a year to come together; in 482 he reinforced the alliance and began clearing raiders from southeastern Talindon, and by 483 his campaign began in earnest, splitting the allied army into two contingents — one under himself, the other under the Sunderland general Waiqar Sumarion — with the consent of Falladir and Triamlavar. At the Battle of the Burning Hills, Timmorran struck down Chief Lukosh with his own magic, breaking the Orcs' loyalty to Llovar's cause. In 487, the Host of Thorns overwhelmed Falladir's forces at The Ashen Field, and Timmorran regrouped the survivors in the foothills of the Razor Crags for a final stand, reinforced unexpectedly by Dwarven troops from Thelgrim under Halgir Son of Grom. In the climactic Battle of the Locusts, Timmorran withstood Llovar's sorcerous assault behind a shield of pure force before piercing his uncle's rotten heart with a lance of arcane power, ending the First Darkness with Llovar's death.

Later Life

Though records of Greyhaven's founding are sketchy, most hold that it was Timmorran himself who founded the northern city, perhaps for refugees lost in the wilderness and those seeking to forget the horrors of the war he had just fought. He later journeyed with an army into the Charg'r Wastes to rescue Waiqar from the ruins of Llovar's Black Citadel, and was instrumental in persuading the Lords of Talindon to crown Baron Daqan king in Llovar's aftermath. In his final years, his obsessive quest to preserve his power for future generations led him to create the Orb of the Sky at the Tower of Meringyr — an ambition that, when Waiqar betrayed him on the Night of Betrayal, cost Timmorran his life.

Legacy

The Stars of Timmorran, unstable magical artifacts capable of powering great magic, are named for him; when the dragonlords of the Third Darkness broke these Stars down into smaller, stable runebound shards, they transformed the nature of magic across Mennara.

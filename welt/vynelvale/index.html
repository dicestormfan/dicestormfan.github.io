Vynelvale

Vynelvale is one of the eight Free Cities of Terrinoth, the monastery city and holiest of them all, located southeast of Riverwatch at the juncture of the Guardian Hills and the Great Plains.

History

According to the current Grand Hierophants of Kellos, soon after the Second Darkness a vile necromancer near Vynelvale sought to create an undead empire for himself. A band of heroes assembled to defeat him but were struck down; the last survivor, moments from a sorcerous death, called out for aid and swore eternal service to any who would come. His eyes clouded, and his body became wreathed in flame; raising a hand that shone bright enough to make the sun seem a flickering candle, the invigorated survivor smote the necromancer with one wave and banished his undead army forever. As his sight returned he knew that a divine power had interceded. He kept the promise he had made and began preaching of the intercession and the god's power; others who remembered tales of cleansing fire from the Second Darkness, and had lit an Eternal Fire in remembrance, soon joined him, and together they established a church to the being he believed had aided him: Kellos. His followers named him the first Hierophant of Kellos, and upon his death he was canonized as the church's first saint; later hierophants added wings, halls, bastions, and other constructions, turning the Grand Cathedral of Kellos into a structure to rival the greatest castles of the Daqan Lords.

Description

Vynelvale is the administrative and educational center devoted to Kellos, God of Life, Flame, and Justice, a popular pilgrimage site housing the greatest library in Terrinoth outside Greyhaven University. Behind its towering walls, hundreds of initiates silently tend gardens and fields while innumerable scribes spend years copying holy texts and other rare documents. It is here that the Priests and Knights of Kellos are trained in the greatest secrets of their god, able to banish the taint of darkness with little more than a touch and beloved by the people they go out to protect with cleansing flames; pilgrims traveling to Vynelvale for healing loudly cheer them as they pass, recognizing their distinctive red and gold colors and the burning hand of Kellos each wears on their armor.

Baruk Gro Urak

The Baruk Gro Urak, or "Red Hand Tribe," is an Orc tribe of The Baruk Range that, unlike most of Mennara's Orcs, often defies the Pillars and the peace treaties with the Humans, conducting bloody raids on unsuspecting victims.

Their culture is a horrifying mix of ritualistic, blood-shedding mysticism and unrepentant violence, and their leadership is firmly based purely on strength and fighting prowess. They have no economy or agriculture, relying entirely on hunting and raids conducted from the natural, barren caves they use as shelter, and are bound more by a shared cultural identity than any interpersonal relationships — with the exception of their mates, most Red Hands will make no meaningful effort to assist another enfeebled or wounded Orc.

It is a culture so merciless and brutal that Baruk Gro Urak Orcs are only considered adults once their hands are permanently dyed by the blood of their enemies. The ritual requires that their skin be abrasively scrubbed with a curak cactus while submerged in the Rakbo River, then emerge still stained a dark, rust-colored crimson. If judged successful by Chieftain Barka, the Orc earns the revered status of Red Hand and the ability to participate in tribal councils; failure means those witnessing the rite beat the Orc viciously and then socially shun the applicant for a full cycle of the moon.

Emorial

Lord Emorial, called Father to the Elves and Lord Protector of Light, was the husband of Latariana whose failed rebellion against The First led to the Elves' exile and imprisonment upon Mennara.

History

When Latariana's covetous longing for a Crown of Dreams and Life could not be denied, Emorial traveled to the farthest edge of creation to demand the First relinquish dominion over the Empyrean's spheres to her; they did not so much as deign to respond, leaving him humiliated and wrathful. He returned to the Elves and marshaled them in mutiny, marching his host with Latariana at his side through The Gate of Years and, unknowingly, into the Aenlong, where after countless days lost among its hazards they came at last to the glade that would become The Aymhelin and met its architect, the Yrthwright Mennara. The dragon passed down the First's judgment: the Elves were exiled from the Empyrean and imprisoned upon the mortal plane, with Latariana alone offered a terrible bargain to keep hope of return alive, at the cost of entering the Void herself.

After Latariana's sacrifice, Emorial divided his host into eleven tribes and shared the Tears of Latariana between eleven Elven nobles as marks of office, directing them to lead the Elves into the future of this new world until a path back to the Empyrean was made clear. Wracked with shame, anger, and grief he could not accept for himself, he then took up his sword and left his people, journeying — the Elves say — to the Ynfernael plane to seek vengeance on the corrupting shadow that had murmured temptations to his bride. He was never seen among the Elves again.

Legacy

Generations later, Malcorne, chieftain of the eleventh tribe, claimed to have encountered Emorial in the Jornall Mountains where the Elves believed he had pursued the Ynfernael's servants; this Emorial supposedly revealed that the First were tyrants and that Mennara's promise to Latariana was a lie meant to keep the Elves subservient forever, charging Malcorne with revealing this truth and leading the tribes in rebellion. The Latari Elves maintain to this day that it was not Emorial whom Malcorne encountered in that cave, but a cunning demon wearing his face — a deception that set in motion The War of the Shadow Tear. The Deep Elves count themselves among those still dedicated to Emorial's true mission against the forces of the Ynfernael, in an effort to redeem the Elven name from its sullying by Malcorne.

GC & RoT

Verto Magica

The Verto Magica, also called "the Turning," is the spiritual mechanism The First set into motion after conjuring the three Planes of Power, involving the rise and fall of the Empyrean and the Ynfernael planes.

Without the Verto Magica, everything would be fixed in place, and time would stand still; learned beings propose that for something to occur, the energy for such an occurrence must exist, and that all such energy is due to the Turning. Timmorran's old tutor Sal Meringyr harbored an ambition to crystallize a measure of the Verto Magica's own energy, a dangerous idea he planted in his young apprentice's head — an ambition that Timmorran carried forward decades later in his creation of the Orb of the Sky.

Necromancers debate whether death magic itself draws upon the Verto Magica, or upon some lower, related form of energy.

True Sorcery

According to texts supposedly penned by Timmorran himself, after the Empyrean and Ynfernael were created, their constant vying with one another for supremacy caused each realm's influence to rise and fall in a cyclical manner — in some ages the dark forces of the Ynfernael were ascendant and sorrow and madness reigned, in others the Empyrean's influence dominated and peace prevailed. This eternal transit between opposing forces created a spinning wheel, the motion termed the Turning; theory holds it to be the force behind the movement of the sun, moon, and stars, the turning of the seasons, and the processes behind the weather — in short, the Verto Magica is the force of all change in the world, the relentless march of time.

It was rare in the days of the Steel Age for one to be born with the gift to harness the Verto Magica directly, and it is rarer still in the current age of uncertainty. This force can be touched by one with the gift, akin to dipping one's fingers in a rushing stream, allowing a "true sorcerer" to draw power from the wellspring or even redirect the flow of the energy itself. The Elves nevertheless consider this "lower magic" compared to their own Empyrean arts, tainted as it is by the influence of the Ynfernael and more destructive and chaotic in its effects; nonetheless there are more Elven true sorcerers than mortal ones, as only Elves have the long life spans necessary to dedicate themselves to mastering the Verto Magica and become powerful storm sorceresses or frost mages even without runebound or unbound shards.

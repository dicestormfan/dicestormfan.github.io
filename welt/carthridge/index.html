Carthridge

Carthridge, the Silver Barony, abuts Rhynn in Northern Terrinoth, extending across the river of Korina's Tears and encompassing the larger part of the Carthmounts.

Taking advantage of abundant farmland and the famed silver mines scattered through the mountains, Carthridge enjoys great wealth and influence — an abundance of food and coin that attracts wild creatures and bandits in equal measure. The people seldom worry, since Baron Zachareth's knights and foot soldiers are among the best-equipped and most trained in Terrinoth, kept in a state of constant readiness by his order despite centuries without serious challenge. Despite the unnatural threats lurking within the Mistlands, Carthridge's people continue to farm the fertile soil that extends to the edges of the shrouded bogs, though life at the doorstep of the Betrayer has taken its toll: they bar their doors at night and mistrust strangers as a matter of course.

Far in Carthridge's northern reaches, outside any barony's control, lies Strangehaven, a lawless den of outcasts and eccentrics where almost anything can be found for a price and few questions are asked; despite occasional Fog Runners tracking undead targets there, the town has so far gone unharmed. In the barony's remote parts, the otherwise unremarkable Lake Falstar holds a single island at its center, home to the seldom-visited Temple of Four Storms, an ancient monument to a long-forgotten religion. Likely owing to Carthridge's ancient border with the Thirteenth Barony, Baron Zachareth is the only baron to speak regularly and emphatically of the threat posed by the Mistlands, agitating for a preemptive invasion he volunteers to lead — an idea the other barons have so far refused to support, with Barons Xarles and Echard privately fearing it would make a hero of its general and upset the council's balance of power.

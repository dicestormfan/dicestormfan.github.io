Stars of Timmorran

The Stars of Timmorran are the shattered fragments of Timmorran's Orb of the Sky, unstable magical artifacts capable of powering great magic in the hands of a trained wizard, named and safeguarded by Lumii Tamar after the Night of Betrayal.

History

The Council of Barons granted Lumii Tamar the authority to found an order of wizards to protect the Stars and advise on their use, but Lumii and the Stars pass from history at that moment. As time passed and a feeling of security returned to Terrinoth, the Stars slowly began to emerge from wherever Lumii and his followers had hidden them, making their way into the treasuries of barons, the laboratories of wizards, and the workings of magical wonders — some even reached the Courts of the Latari, the Deeplord Halls of Dunwarr, and the Throne Room of the Lorimor Empire.

Under the extravagant reign of the Soulstone Kings, the degree of secrecy that had once surrounded the Stars broke down entirely; they were traded for gold and used to engineer public works and spectacles throughout the Time of the Elder Kings, a wondrous era of trade and cooperation between the Dunwarr, the Latari, the Lorimor Empire, and Terrinoth that the Latari themselves instead call "The Days before the Dragons," for their overt use drew the attention of the dragonlords and brought about the Third Darkness. During that war, the Dragonlord Zir the Black discovered how to break the Stars down into smaller fragments inscribed with runes — runebound shards, smaller and more stable artifacts usable even by a bearer with no magical knowledge — transforming the very nature of magic across Mennara and turning Margath's host from a formidable army into one that was nigh unbeatable.

Legacy

Greyhaven scholars believe the Orb of the Sky was the most powerful magical artifact that ever existed, storing enough magic to have allowed later wizards to cast billions upon billions of potent spells without needing to tap into the Verto Magica, sparing Terrinoth from ever fearing invasion again — that promise died with the Orb, leaving only the Stars. [not stated in source: an explicit causal link is not drawn, but the source places the destruction of the Orb and the birth of the Undying One together as] the first horrific repercussion of Timmorran's actions, with one Star said to have been associated with the birth of the Undying One.

For generations the Stars were kept secured and secret, but they grew widespread and conspicuous in the time of the Elder Kings, drawing the dragons' notice; for reasons still unknown, the dragons emerged from the Molten Heath and raged across Mennara in search of Stars, so that Timmorran's Orb brought about a second, even more devastating war on top of the first. The dragons fractured their captured Stars into runebound shards, which could only produce a single effect each but were far easier to operate than a full Star — more useful for the dragons and their hybrid minions.

The use of runebound shards spread across Mennara after the war ended, even though most Stars were never found. The dragons are believed to have amassed an enormous hoard, but no one knows its eventual fate; nevertheless, so many shards were created that untold numbers remain. Some fell into the hands of the defenders during the Third Darkness, and small caches of others have been found in the wilderness in the centuries since. Powerful barons, military armories, and wealthy collectors possess most of them today, and Greyhaven University and the cult of Kellos each maintain their own private reserves.

A few Stars are thought to have escaped the dragons entirely. Even the slightest hint of their existence is enough to launch legends, such as the Sunderlander's Stone, the Vault of Jade Twilight, and the Chasm of Stars.

Priests of Pollux

The Priests of Pollux, followers of the mysterious divine artificer Pollux, maintain a large cult in Nerekhall, and in the aftermath of a member of the city's Council of Wizards opening a portal to the Ynfernael, they created the Ironbound to protect the city from ever falling to that corruption again.

Description

No one knows who Pollux was, or what they did in life — there are no stories of great deeds or heroic adventures that accompany the name. Instead, Pollux comes from a series of treatises and diagrams discovered by artificers during the time of the Elder Kings, each describing some wondrous construction or artifact, or providing the formula for some alchemical creation or magical reagent. Each was written on thin sheets of steel and signed with the name "Pollux," and those artificers who followed the instructions claimed the designs must have been inspired by the divine; each treatise quickly came to be treated as a holy relic. The Artificers of Pollux do not work miracles through prayer as the disciples of Kellos do; instead they work their magic through feats of engineering, studying enchantments alongside more mundane skills such as carpentry or blacksmithing. Their holy sites are workshops and forges, and their sacred writings are treatises on natural elements and recipes for rare reagents — a prayer to Pollux is a call for inspiration and ingenuity to craft the seemingly impossible.

Artificers are regarded with both affection and suspicion by the people of Terrinoth. On one hand, the followers of Pollux have produced a number of revered artifacts used in civic works and military operations, including the Ironbound that combat evil magic-users stalking the underbelly of Nerekhall. Yet the devout keep their formulae close, sharing their knowledge only with those who swear themselves to secrecy, and some of the Runemasters of Greyhaven — jealous of the artificers' achievements — have spread unsavory rumors about the true source of their wonders, rumors the Artificers of Pollux decry as unfounded slander, insisting they bear a responsibility to ensure Pollux's legacy doesn't end up in the wrong hands.

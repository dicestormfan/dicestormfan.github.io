The Mistlands

The Mistlands are the cursed domain of Waiqar the Undying, the shrouded, ever-shifting lands of the thirteenth barony of Terrinoth, whose seat at Archaut has remained empty for hundreds of generations.

Those who dare travel beyond Greyhaven find themselves in a landscape where warming light seldom reaches the ground, bounded by fetid marshes to the west and the Dunwarr Mountains to the north. The region is filled with volcanic crags and sprawling marshlands that bubble and boil, expelling murderous gases and trapping foolish adventurers in their black muck for eternity. Nearly impassable since Waiqar's betrayal so many hundreds of years ago, the Mistlands serve as the base from which he launches his dark campaigns to raze Terrinoth and gain control over the runebound shards. As his power has grown over the centuries, the Mistlands have spread beyond the original bounds of the Thirteenth Barony, and inexorably continue to extend ever farther into the lands of the living.

Notable places within the Mistlands include Waiqar's holdfast of Zorgas, the vampiric domain of Bilehall and its subject Castle Dalibor, the haunted The Misty Hills, and the border territory of Roth's Vale.

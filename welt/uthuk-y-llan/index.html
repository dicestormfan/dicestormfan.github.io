Uthuk Y'llan

The Uthuk Y'llan are the corrupted, demon-worshipping raiders of The Ru, once peaceful plains-dwellers of the Loth K'har transformed into a hungry horde of locusts when Llovar fell prey to the Ynfernael.

History

On the Night of Summons, Llovar led his tribe's greatest warriors into the T'mara T'rusheen with promises of great rewards and forbidden bindings; they emerged physically transformed by Ynfernael influence, wiry muscles grown supernaturally powerful and their very bones pushed through their skin to form great spikes and blades. With these warriors at his side, Llovar formed a confederation of tribes, conquering those who refused to ally willingly — even the chiefs of the Scal and L'jan tribes, many times larger than the Loth K'har, bent the knee. The campaign bloomed into full civil war between the tribes of the plains, and the Loth K'har and their allies took on the new title of Uthuk Y'llan, "locust swarm" in the language of the plains people, as they conquered more and more of the Ru — a name earned in earnest as Llovar's Host of Thorns went on to ravage Talindon during the First Darkness.

Overview

To traverse the Darklands is to enter the world of the Uthuk Y'llan and their Ynfernael allies. Here, the tribes rule the cursed earth beneath the baleful light cast from The Spire of Ruin — the midnight heart of the Darklands themselves. There are many Uthuk tribes, each grown from splinters of Llovar's host, yet a few stand above the rest in size and ferocity.

Tribes

The Gore Claws are the largest and most aggressive of the Uthuk, descendants of the prophet Golreth, who keep the legends of Llovar and the Ynfernael masters alive and strive to unite the tribes as the self-proclaimed inheritors of the Locust Swarm.

The Flayed Kindred do not covet rulership of the Ru like the Gore Claws; instead they seek only to make themselves closer to the power of the Ynfernael realm, and hold many of the keys left in the wake of Llovar's demise, including those believed to open the gates to the Black Citadel.

The Night Howlers hold territories close to the borderlands and are among the Uthuk's most skilled raiders, having perfected the art of night fighting from the depths of Nyak Sutchra'aa, the Chasm of Eternal Night.

Beyond these three largest tribes, scores of others roam the Ru Darklands, dwelling in blighted caves, crumbling temples, and profane ruins such as the Tower of Hands, the Ynargal Cathedral, and the Spinespirit Caves. Only the barest information on the major Uthuk settlements exists, for few outsiders ever see them and live. Captives taken to the Breeding Pits of Yyrg are hurled into bone cages to be possessed by Ynfernael spirits and transformed into beasts of war. Only slightly less harrowing than the pits is the Well of Sins, deep within the Gyrar Abyss, where the Uthuk come to trade slaves and souls. The greatest Uthuk settlement is the circle of war camps around Kaylor Morbis, where the bloodsisters now hold court, settling disputes via ritual combat, gathering their forces, and planning invasions of other lands.

Castle Dalibor

Castle Dalibor is the seat of House Dalibor within The Mistlands, nominally subservient to Waiqar though somewhat less powerful than Bilehall, currently led by the vampire Kyndrithul.

Description

Despite the fear and awe that any tyrant inspires, there always seems to be one general, one advisor, to whom the offer of power means nothing — they serve not because they wish to, but because they have been forced to. Kyndrithul is one such figure, a powerful vampire who is at heart an experimenter, a genius who has spent centuries mastering the various magical disciplines needed to create the perfect Reanimated servant. The apparent subservience Castle Dalibor displays belies its strength.

His estate is filled with all manner of unnatural creatures: the surrounding forests are choked with roving packs of barghests, while bone horrors and flesh constructs shamble aimlessly in search of prey. Kyndrithul's favorite saying, ever the pragmatist, is that all things have their use. Any heroes unfortunate enough to be taken prisoner in Castle Dalibor soon find themselves in his laboratory, where he exults in figuring out new ways to mold living flesh with necromancy. His most recent, boldest experiments infuse living flesh with the dust of Waiqar's own Deathborn Legion — a crime punishable with a fate beyond death, terrible enough to make even Kyndrithul tremble at the thought of discovery.

Despite the immense risk of these experiments, he nonetheless offers Waiqar aided passage through his lands and space to establish staging points for raids into Terrinoth. While Waiqar's Deathborn generals look upon the vampire with disgust and suspicion, Kyndrithul knows that as long as he plays the part of a humble servant, he may yet reap the rewards of his secret work.

Somewhere within the Mistlands, deep below the volcanic caves under the ruins of Kelipa, grinds The Everliving Engine — its exact relation to Castle Dalibor is not stated in the source, but it is described alongside it.

Nordros

Nordros, Master of Cold and Death, is the icy God of Cold and Death, believed by the Weik of Isheim to be their eternal enemy, continually trying to blow them back into the sea with his icy breath — the harsh elements that seek their deaths at every turn.

Weik Belief

Nordros's worship is banned among the Weik. Each year upon the longest day, the twelve Hearthlords of the Reaver Council come to Lagart to spit upon his broken statue and reaffirm their mastery over their land. There is even a legend that tells of a mighty warrior who will one day vanquish Nordros, a glory the Weik who train within the heroes' halls dream might be theirs. The Weik say Nordros long predates Waiqar, coming down from the north at the dawn of the world with blizzards roaring at his heels, kept at bay by the doughty warriors of Isheim, but returning each year when the sun grows weak; the Weik believe that if Nordros is not kept in check, he will finally gain dominion over the realms and see the entire world encased in ice.

Kellos's Church and Nordros

Some temples of Kellos teach a very different account: that Nordros is Kellos's own twin brother, whose jealousy of his sibling led him to fall to Waiqar's dark promises during the Second Darkness. When Nordros joined the Betrayer, he unlocked a terrible power within himself, a mastery over the chill of death, and the temple faithful hold that Kellos's church teaches the undead are Nordros's agents — as the twin flames of justice and hope flicker and dim, Nordros's power grows, and only when he is defeated will Kellos return and winter be forever banished from the realms.

The worship of Nordros is almost unheard of in Terrinoth, with only a few pockets remaining in the Howling Giant Hills and Blind Muir Forest, where some still bring sacrifices to appease him; a few ambitious individuals in Strangehaven pledge themselves to Nordros in pursuit of dominion over cold and death, some counting on their resolve to use the magic of ice and death without succumbing to the god's will. The inhabitants of cities like Vynelvale and Dawnsmoor actively persecute any who openly follow Nordros, and the cult of Kellos continues to petition the Citadel to ban his worship outright.

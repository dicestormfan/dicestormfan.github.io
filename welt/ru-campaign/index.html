Ru Campaign

The Ru Campaign was Waiqar Sumarion's war against Da'Roul Bonesplicer and the remnants of the Uthuk Y'llan, undertaken at the urging of King Daqan after Waiqar's long recuperation from his imprisonment in Llovar's Black Citadel.

Once confident and brash, Waiqar had grown cold and calculating from his ordeal, but he still managed to win glory for himself, vanquishing Da'Roul and annihilating the Uthuk camps throughout the Ru and the Darklands. Daqan ordered the building of guard towers along the borders of the forbidden lands to watch for signs of resurgent Uthuk, though in the years since the watchtowers fell into disuse, so thoroughly had the Uthuk been destroyed. In the hour of his triumph, Waiqar nonetheless complained to his captains of Timmorran's complacency and arrogance, insisting that many soldiers' lives could have been saved had the wizard augmented their efforts with magic — an early sign of the resentment that would later curdle into betrayal.

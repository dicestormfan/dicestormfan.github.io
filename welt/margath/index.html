Margath

Margath the Unkind was the dragonlord who led the invasion of Terrinoth during the Third Darkness, ultimately slain by Lord Roland of Kell at the war's culmination.

History

In 1024, Margath was one of six dragonlords — alongside Levirax, Baalesh, Zir the Black, Avox, and Gehennor — who each led a band of lesser drakes against a different part of the world, motivated by greed for the Stars of Timmorran; the attacks were nearly simultaneous but uncoordinated between the rival dragonlords, none of whom were acting with the sanction of the Great Dragon Rex Shaarina. Margath knew from the outset what he wanted and where to get it: a great cache of Stars held in the treasury of King Trevnor Soulstone in Tamalir. His host flew to the Free City and faced little opposition, his dragon hybrids descending on its towers and artillery while his drakes incinerated the infantry sent against them; Margath himself is said to have broken into the royal palace and burned the king to charred meat along with his family and retainers, leaving Terrinoth without a ruler once more and gathering up the palace's riches, including a sizable cache of Stars.

As the war dragged on and the dragonlords turned Stars into weaponized runebound shards, Margath's host became nigh unbeatable, so confident in its new power that he ceased managing it as a coherent force, instead letting each dragonlord raid independently. In 1029, in the war's final days, Lord Roland of Kell led the defense of the city of Kell using a ruse — false rumors of Stars hidden there — to lure Margath's forces into a hail of ballista fire from the city's defensive redoubts. As the dragons retreated to the cold skies above the Mountains of Despair, Roland took to the air on his winged Yeron and summoned freezing rain onto Margath's position with an ice storm runebound shard, leaving the dragonlord dazed and weakened from the cold; Roland then drove his lance through the great drake's neck, killing him. In his death throes, Margath broke the wings of Roland's mount, and both riders tumbled from the sky to their deaths — their remains were never recovered from the snow-shrouded peaks.

[not stated in source: a separate account, tied to the cult of Aris, instead holds that Margath's body fell to earth at the Weeping Basin and was slain by a "Baron Rolan." The two accounts are not reconciled in the sources and are recorded here as each source states them.]

Lithelin

Lithelin is the heart of the Elven empire, a gleaming city of white towers rising proudly in the heart of the ancient forest of The Aymhelin, ruled from its High Court by King Aenoeth.

Overview

The palace and the oldest rings of Lithelin are isolated from the opulent, verdant bowl of vigorous forest green that surrounds the city, but its outer districts have grown into, through, and among the forest in a graceful merging of Elven architecture and natural beauty. At its center stands Caelcira, the political and spiritual heart of the Aymhelin, and nearby lies the Ailatar, the Plain of Stars where the Elves descended to Mennara.

At the head of the High Court sits King Aenoeth, an Elf with a centuries-long reputation as a fair and benevolent ruler; at his side is his trusted advisor and consort Maegan Cyndewin, viewed with suspicion by much of the court despite her own valor in defending the city.

Description

In the center of Lithelin is Caelcira, or "First Gate," which has watched over the city for over four thousand years, standing proud against the assaults of Dragonlord Baalesh and his fiery brood; it is said Caelcira is built on the founding stone of the world itself and will never fall to evil, unless all of Mennara shall fall first. Here the Tear of the Latari tribe and those of the lost nations are guarded carefully, lest they fall into the hands of evil. Although the Tears are officially named for Latariana, an old adage among the Eolam court holds that her tears actually fall again due to the innumerable intrigues, plots, and jealousies that plague the court.

Not all politicking in the High Court is guileful, however. Maegan Cyndewin, though she fought valiantly and was wounded repeatedly while defending the Elven nation, is still deeply distrusted by many in the Court and conspired against whenever possible — a rueful hatred fueled in part by the legacy of her father, a member of the Daewyl tribe that allied with demons in its covetous pursuit of Latariana's Tears, and by the legacy of her mother, a Loth Caara tribeswoman who lived to see Llovar corrupt her people. Her parentage serves as a perpetual blight to her honor despite how instrumental she was in defending Caelcira, and many fear she has been corrupted by the same power that seduced her parents' tribes and is working to bring about the same downfall of the Latari.

Between the palace and the walls lie swaths of verdant lands interspersed with pure, spring-fed ponds, wild-growing wheat, and panon-fruit trees delicately transplanted from the surrounding forests. Originally created as groves for the Forest Guardians who lend aid in defense of Caelcira, these vales have become the spiritual hub of Lithelin, home to a dizzying array of shrines, prayer gardens, and temples dedicated to the Path of the Stars, maintained by High Priestess Ancela, and to sanctums for the Wealdworkers and Dreamweavers. Beyond these vales, in towering spires, reside the powerful storm sorceresses.

Hidden far behind the borders of the green lands, obscured from the view of any travelers who may venture to or from the palace, are the jarring, uncharacteristically dilapidated homes of the Verdelam, Elves of lower blood desperate to prove themselves worthy of social ascension. Each morning they journey to the keep and surrounding temples to perform the mundane tasks and menial labor that keep the kingdom functioning smoothly, allowing them — at a pace that would mortify any of the world's shortlived races — to slowly earn the respect and prestige necessary to rise to meaningful posts within Elven society. Those who fail to ascend but are unwilling or unable to return to their former lives usually embrace the life of an adventurer or join the ranks of the Latari army; while noble blood is required to become an officer, Captain Ilendar accepts into the rank and file all who can meet the physical requirements of soldiering.

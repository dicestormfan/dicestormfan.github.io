Runemaster

Runemasters are practitioners of a form of magic that has come to dominate the esteemed halls of Greyhaven since the end of the Third Darkness, dispensing with many of the risks taken by true sorcerers since they do not tap into the Verto Magica in such a direct fashion.

Description

Most Runemasters are not born with the gift of sorcery and are thus unable to cast magic on their own; instead of working their own feats of magic, they activate the latent energies of runebound shards or manipulate the runes graven onto their surface to shift or focus the intended effect closer to their desires. Although anyone can activate a runebound shard, to become a Runemaster a person must learn the meanings of the runes and the manner in which the dragons originally inscribed them, a study that takes years, if not decades, to master — by tracing the runes in a subtly different pattern, or focusing on specific claw strokes, a Runemaster can direct a shard's energies, omit certain effects, or change the nature of their expression, typically increasing the duration, degree, range, or targets of an effect. In extremely rare cases, Runemasters may modify a runebound shard itself, erasing or adding certain claw strokes to change its base effect — though no matter their skill, the most fundamental elements of a rune are irrevocably branded onto the shard, meaning it cannot be erased and an unbound shard reclaimed, nor can its element or affinity be changed to a wholly different sphere: a rune of immolation might become a rune of blasting or a rune of flame, but never a rune of ice storm or of teleportation.

The University of Greyhaven is the official home of the Runemasters, where tutors carefully instruct apprentices and research new methods of rune manipulation, instilling not only correct technique but also an unstinting loyalty to fellow Runemasters; senior Runemasters carefully maintain good relations with the Daqan Lords and other powerful establishments of Terrinoth, though some critics accuse them of playing a long, cynical game to oust practitioners of true sorcery in favor of their own limited but safer practices. Runemasters are among the sole persons capable of awakening the Rune Golems that protect the realms of Terrinoth in times of great need, and some are rumored to be artificing new creatures empowered by runebound shards, such as rune steeds like Baron Zachareth's Backbreaker. Other communities of Runemasters exist besides Greyhaven's, including one in the Lorimor Empire that closely follows Greyhaven's model; as the Dragon Wars were primarily confined to Terrinoth, only a handful of runebound shards are known to have crossed the great sea to Al-Kalim.

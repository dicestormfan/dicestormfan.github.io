The Ugluk Badlands

In a region filled with inhospitable terrain, the Ugluk Badlands are perhaps the worst of The Broken Plains. With rocky peaks and flash floods that produce new ravines, the area is rough and unforgiving; game is scarce and successful hunts are hard won, and the air is dusty with a thick, earthy odor that few find appealing, or even tolerable.

The vegetation is as harsh as the land, but often can be rendered and treated to produce effective — if extremely foul-tasting and painful in application — medicines and poultices that some claim can defeat death itself with the correct preparation. Many Orcs who travel to Terrinoth claim to be from the area, if for no other reason than to impress the terrible nature of the Broken Plains onto others.

Being in the interior of the Broken Plains, the Badlands see few visitors. Some travelers pass through here on their way to the narrow passage into the Ru to the north, in quests that later few speak of, as there are few survivors. Some come for the unique medicinal ingredients to be harvested, which can fetch more gold than lesser runebound shards in some cases. For those expert in the saddle, though, it is a place to find the Mag Ugluk Urak riders and their famed reptilian steeds, the Gurak Tol.

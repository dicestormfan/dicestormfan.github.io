Latari

The Latari are the Elves of The Aymhelin, a nation ruled from Lithelin whose civilization has been carefully harmonized with the ancient forest around it over millennia.

Overview

The Latari follow the Path of the Stars, venerating Latariana, and trace their society back to their descent to Mennara at The Ailatar. Elven society is organized around castes and tribes: the ruling houses of the Eolam court, the lower-blood Verdelam, and tribes such as the Daewyl and Loth Caara, whose historical corruption still colors High Court politics. All Latari, wherever they live, fiercely guard the forests from outside intrusion, lest contaminations disrupt the Elves' eternal quest to return to the Empyrean.

Description

Beyond Lithelin, Latari society spreads across the whole of the Aymhelin: the frontier warriors of Blackthorn Grove, the foresters and hunters of The Bloodwood, the seafarers and spiritspeakers of the Selenic Coast, the moonstone miners and Swordsmasters of The Darnati Highlands, the guardians of The Deepwood, and the farmers of The Thalian Glades each represent a distinct facet of Elven life, bound together by shared reverence for nature and vigilance against the many unnatural threats that test the borders of their ancient homeland.

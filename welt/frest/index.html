Frest

Frest, Barony of the Usurped Throne, is unique among the baronies in being ruled not by a hereditary noble but by a first-generation baroness, Harriet the Willful.

Some decades ago, Harriet was a simple carpenter; when the previous baroness abruptly abdicated, Frest fell into chaos that hit the peasantry hardest, until Harriet united her fellow peasants against the bickering nobles and assumed leadership herself. Since her appointment by the will of the people, she has ruled magnanimously from Aerendor Keep: farmers keep what they like and profit from the rest, and Frest has the lowest taxes in all Terrinoth. Its people till the fields and hunt and gather from the serene Applewood Forest, though all know to avoid the Bloodwood on the barony's eastern edge, where the vengeful Elves have claimed few victims in recent years.

The other barons did not take Harriet's rise well and went to war with Frest; the war raged for years until she was finally accepted onto the Council of Thirteen, largely because the war was going poorly for the other barons. Even now the barons fear their own people might follow Frest's example, and as Harriet ages and succession is discussed, some worry that her daughter inheriting the throne would legitimize a precedent for commoners to ennoble themselves or unseat barons entirely. Wary of open action against Harriet risking another war or inciting revolt elsewhere, Baroness Magrit of Dhernas and Baron Gillian of Cailn have instead begun quietly seeking information that might turn Frest's people against their ruler.
